Account deletion

Delete your LectureXP account

LectureXP includes in-app account deletion. This permanently deletes your profile and study data associated with the account.

Delete from the App

  • Open LectureXP.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Open Account.
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Confirm the deletion prompt.

What Is Deleted

  • Your LectureXP profile and app preferences.
  • Your lecture library, generated recall questions, notes, review history, recommendations, and progress records.
  • Uploaded audio files associated with your account, where present.

What May Remain Temporarily

Some provider logs, security records, backups, or transaction records may remain for a limited period where required for fraud prevention, security, accounting, legal compliance, or Apple subscription handling.

Subscriptions

Deleting your LectureXP account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions.

If You Cannot Access the App

Email ikorkod@gmail.com and include the Apple relay email or account identifier you used, if available.

Account deletion is irreversible once completed.
