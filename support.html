Support

Need help with LectureXP?

For support, subscription questions, account deletion issues, or bug reports, contact the developer by email.

Email: ikorkod@gmail.com

Developer: Pavel Korkodinov

What to Include

  • The email or Apple relay address used for Sign in with Apple, if relevant.
  • Your app version and iOS version.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • For lecture processing issues: the lecture or playlist URL, if you can share it.
  • For subscription issues: whether the purchase was weekly or yearly and whether Restore Purchases helped.

Subscriptions

Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple. You can manage subscriptions in iOS Settings -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app at Settings -> Account -> Delete account. See Account deletion for details.

We usually respond as soon as practical.
